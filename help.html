How much does CineStager cost?
CineStager is free to download, and every feature is free to use for 7 days. After that, a one-time purchase of €19.99 (or the equivalent in your country's App Store) unlocks the app for good. There is no subscription.

Your Shot Library stays open after the trial, so your captures are always there.
Which modes does CineStager have?
CineStager has three modes, and your camera, lens, scene and sun settings go with you when you switch.

Viewfinder is a director's viewfinder: your device's own cameras, ultra-wide included, framed as your cine camera and lens would see it — no location needed. AR places a scanned location around you at 1:1 scale. Game lets you fly a virtual camera through a location with joysticks, anywhere.
How do I get a Location model?
Scan it with CineStager's built-in scanner on an iPhone or iPad with LiDAR. You choose between three kinds of scan: LiDAR (a precise model, ready in minutes), Gaussian splats (photoreal, trained on your device), or Hybrid (the LiDAR model up close, with splats for everything beyond the scanner's reach).

You can also import any USDZ file — including models made in Blender, SketchUp or Unreal Engine, so you can plan shots in a location that hasn't been built yet — or Gaussian splats from a .ply file. LiDAR apps such as Polycam and Scaniverse export USDZ too.
Which devices can use Gaussian splats and Hybrid scans?
Splats need an iPhone or iPad with LiDAR, iOS 27, and an A14 / M1 chip or newer. Training happens on your device while CineStager is open; a large room takes a while, so keep the app in front until it's done. On devices that can't show splats, the Location Library says so.
How do I share a location with someone else?
Export it as a CineStager Location (.csloc): one file with the model, its splats, floors, geography and scenes. Send it with AirDrop, Messages or Files, and open it on the other device to add it to the Location Library.
How do I set up a building with several floors?
When you import a Location model, CineStager opens it so you can set the floor: move the coloured plane up or down until it sits on the floor, and tap Add Floor for every storey above it. You can change them later with Floors in the location's preview.

In AR and Game mode, the floor button switches storeys — the location and your mannequins move with it.
How do I save a scene and use it again?
With a location and at least one mannequin placed, tap LOC and choose Save Scene, then give it a name. Your scenes are under Scenes in the Location Library, grouped by location.

Pick one to set it up again. In AR, tap the floor to place it and the mannequins return to their spots.
How does Sunseeker know where the sun will be?
Give a location its place on earth and north once, in the location's geography. Sunseeker then draws the sun's path over your shot for any date and time — in AR, in Game mode on the scanned location, and in Viewfinder mode by the device's compass. The date and time you pick stay the same when you switch modes.
How accurate is the depth of field?
It's calculated from the focal length, the stop, the sensor and the real distances in your location, so it matches what a real camera would give — we've compared it side by side with a cinema camera. Lenses open to the f-stop they're made for, and T-stops are converted.

In Game mode depth of field works on any device. In AR it uses the LiDAR scanner's depth, so it needs an iPhone or iPad with LiDAR. Focus on a mannequin and it stays sharp, wherever you move it.
What is the film look on RAW photos?
On an iPhone Pro, Viewfinder mode can save a ProRAW photo with each still. The Shot Library gives these a cinematic film look, which you can adjust: exposure, white balance and tint, and 8, 16 or 35 mm film grain. You can share the photo with the look, or the original DNG.
Something is missing from my library. How do I get it back?
If CineStager finds location models, photos or videos that aren't listed in your library, the library shows a notice with a Recover button that adds them back. On a new device, your library can take a moment to appear while it downloads from iCloud.
Why does my Location model look flat?
CineStager disables Apple's environmental lighting and uses a white HDRi for consistency — this makes LiDAR scans look their best. If you import a model from Blender, SketchUp or Unreal Engine, it may look flat as a result. To fix this, bake lighting into the textures of your model before exporting.
Why can't I choose a wider lens in AR mode?
Apple only allows the main camera to be used for AR content. When using wider focal lengths, a gray border appears around the live camera feed. This is an Apple platform limitation, not a CineStager limitation.

For ultra-wide framing, use Viewfinder mode: it uses all of your device's cameras.
Do videos get a map?
Yes. After recording, the map shows the camera's route and how people moved. Shot without a location? Link one afterwards in the Shot Library and the video gets its map.
Why is camera X not included?
If you'd like to see additional cameras or profiles, send us a message and we'll do our best to add it in the next update.
