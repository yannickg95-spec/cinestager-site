01 · Scan

Bring the location
home with you.

Scan during the recce with CineStager's built-in LiDAR scanner. Choose a precise LiDAR model, photoreal Gaussian splats, or Hybrid: the LiDAR model up close, and splats for everything beyond the scanner's reach — the street outside the window, the far end of the warehouse.

Prefer your own tools? Import any USDZ — even a set that only exists in Blender, SketchUp or Unreal — or a splat .ply.

Clip: orbit around a Hybrid scan (LiDAR room + splat exterior)

LiDAR

Fast, precise geometry
  • Ready in minutes, accurate to scale
  • Works on every LiDAR iPhone and iPad
  • Stops where the scanner's range ends

Hybrid

The best of both
  • Solid LiDAR floors and walls to place people on
  • Photoreal splats for everything further away
  • Splats take time to train

Gaussian splats

Photoreal, as the eye sees it
  • Real textures, reflections and light
  • Trained on your iPhone — no cloud
  • Needs iOS 27 and an A14/M1 chip or newer

A library of locations.

  • iCloud sync between iPhone and iPad

Depth of field
you can trust.

Focal length, stop, sensor and the real distances in your location give a true-to-life depth of field — in Game mode on any device, and in AR from the LiDAR scanner's depth. Tested side by side against a real cinema camera.

Focus on a mannequin and it stays sharp, wherever you move it.

Lens character.

One lens button adds the look of the glass: vintage vignette and softness, swirly bokeh, soap-bubble highlights and the stretch of an anamorphic. See how that 1970s set renders your location before you book it.

03 · Light

Know where
the sun will be.

Sunseeker draws the sun's path over your shot for any date and time. In AR and Viewfinder it works anywhere, straight away — tap the real sun to set north exactly. In Game mode it follows your scanned location. Find the golden-hour window, the backlight, the moment the shadow leaves the doorway.

Pick a date and time once; every mode keeps it.

04 · Block

Put people
in the frame.

Eight people in five poses — standing, walking, sitting, on the ground and lying — each with their own skin, hair, top and bottom colours. Set their height, place them on the right floor, and switch poses without losing their mark.

In AR, people occlusion lets you block real actors inside the virtual location.

Capture & share

From previs
to the whole crew.

Every capture keeps what you set up — camera, lens, stop, positions — and arrives with a map your gaffer, AD and DP can read.

RAW with a film look.

In Viewfinder mode, save an Apple ProRAW DNG with every photo. The Shot Library gives it a cinematic film look: fine-tune exposure, white balance and tint, add 8, 16 or 35 mm grain — then share the photo, the film-look JPEG or the original DNG.

Maps for every shot.

Each still saves a top-down map with the camera and every person on it. Videos get one too, with the camera's route and how people moved. Shot something without a location — even in Viewfinder mode? Link one afterwards in the Shot Library.

  • Clean video: mannequins, location, depth of field and framelines — without the buttons. Sound optional
  • Camera, lens, stop and distances in every file's metadata
  • Shots and maps appear in CinePlanner automatically, through iCloud
